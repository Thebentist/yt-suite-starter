# Voice guide: thebentist (Dr. Ben Winters)

Written 2026-09-27 from 80 whisper transcripts: 43 long-form (mostly reactions) and 37 Shorts. Double quotes are verbatim Ben with whisper's punctuation, not clip audio. *Italics* are phrase names. *n of 43* counts long-form videos (my grep). About 25 Shorts are cuts of long videos, so *of 80* shares run high.

## How he opens (first 20 seconds)
- **Weekly reaction (23 of 43):** his name and an aside about the week: "What's up everybody? I'm Dr. Winters and guess what? School is almost out, isn't it?" (`GFqZ9Ej2xKo`)
- **Compilation (7):** the title promise comes first: "So these are the craziest dental transformation videos on the internet right now" (`9JjBrR_e_GU`)
- **Explainer:** "What if I told you that losing teeth is no longer permanent?" (`NGOf5DmEFvA`)

Then the credential, as a job and not a CV: "Listen, I'm a dentist. I look at teeth all day" (`AN4K7xY__BQ`). He's out within about three sentences with *let's get into it*.

## How he explains
- **One household object, reused across videos:** "Think almost like termites eating into wood." (`_Y8IIXbfGA0`) Also "So it's kind of like cement." (`s4NUrJIh_bc`) (*almost like* 12 of 43, *kind of like* 16)
- **He names it:** "So this is what we call a calculus bridge there in the bottom." (`s4NUrJIh_bc`) (*what we call* 25 of 43)

- **The consequence lands on you:** "Because if you don't get an implant, that bone will just dissolve on you." (`GFqZ9Ej2xKo`)

## How he reacts

- **Kind to the person:** "But luckily, this guy is just an actor and those teeth are just an act." (`q8regjBdFxo`)

## How he moves between beats
Sentence openers: And 12.7%, So 8.2%, I 6.8%.
- *first off* (23): "Okay, first off, hold up." (`Tr2K_faDjR8`)
- *either way* (20): "Either way, they look cool." (`GFqZ9Ej2xKo`)
- *and guess what* (17): "And guess what? I started crying." (`3glVMZtfYOU`)
- Also *in fact* (26), *anyways* (18) and *that being said* (15). Between clips he says *all right what do we got here* (14).

He ends a beat on *but yeah* or *so yeah* (25 and 24), or on an instruction: "So make sure you floss. It's really important." (`GFqZ9Ej2xKo`)

*What if I told you* is mostly a mid-video pivot (6 of the 8 videos that use it): "But what if I told you that I've seen teeth that look like this, not because of Dr. Pepper" (`q8regjBdFxo`)

## How he closes
In 39 of 43, the last 110 words contain *see you*: next week (25), in the next one (10), next time (4). The formula: "If you enjoyed it, you know what to do. I'll see you in the next one. Peace." (`s4NUrJIh_bc`) Explainers ask a real question first: "I don't know, let me know down in the comments below." (`NGOf5DmEFvA`) Shorts have no sign-off and stop on the advice: "So probably don't do that." (`zoOX-bB1WPU`)

## What the top fingerprint phrases really are
- **10 million (31 of 80) is a recurring bit, not voice.** In 30 videos it's his subscribe joke about the bracket on his tooth. It runs as a mid-roll insert in 12 videos, "When this channel hits 10 million subscribers, I'm going to take this bracket off live on camera." (`AN4K7xY__BQ`), or as an outro, "But you know what else is crazy? The fact that you haven't subscribed yet" (`GFqZ9Ej2xKo`). The one literal hit is "We had 10 million views" (`3glVMZtfYOU`). Whisper writes *dollars* three times (not checked by ear).
- **on there (38 of 80)** is real. It's play-by-play of material going onto a tooth: "putting some etch and gel on there" (`PrEgcm82LRI`)
- **and stuff (40 of 80)** is real. It's his list closer after one or two items: "biting your nails and stuff like that" (`DY7ZtvZBgCw`). With *things like that*, it replaces triplets.
- **i'm not gonna** is *I'm not gonna lie* (49 of 55 hits), an admission.
- **Plugs:** no third-party sponsor reads. His own toothpaste comes up in 25 of 80: "That's actually why we created the first ever biotech toothpaste in the world" (`irSK2gbcOm4`). Script plugs and the bracket bit only if Ben asks.

## Rhythm targets (long-form, fingerprint)
- **Sentence length:** mean 13.2 words, median 9 (p10 3, p90 26).
- **Sentence mix:** fragments 18.7%, 25+ words 11.6%, questions 9.1%, exclamations 0.1%.
- **Contractions:** 78.2%. **Pace:** 231 wpm.
- **Pronouns per 1k words:** *you/your* 41.7 (others 19.7), *I/me/my* 29.9 (others 18.2), *we/us/our* 10.2 (others 18.4). His *we* usually means dentists: "We call that an oral microbiome." (`RVXQmWSSfAo`)

## Words and moves he does NOT use
*turns out* appears in 2 of 43 (Cleo Abram 64%), *which means* in 6 (52%), *incredibly* in 3 (48%) and *sort of* in 3 (32%). He says *kind of* in 42. *here's the thing*, *in other words*, *therefore*, *walk you through*, *no shot* and *hits different* never appear. *Imagine* scales a consequence rather than setting a scene: "So imagine what your white sink is going to look like." (`s4NUrJIh_bc`) He doesn't build triplets and doesn't end a beat on an aphorism.

## Proposed updates to the locked voice (needs Ben's OK)
1. Add thought starters *First off*, *Either way*, *Anyways*, *That being said* and *In fact*. Mark *So this leads us to* and *let me explain* as rare.
2. Make *That's crazy* the default reaction. Add *Oh my goodness*, *Holy moly* and *It's almost like* sarcasm. Demote *That is insane* and *Crazy fact*.
3. Add the hedges *more than likely*, *my guess is*, *I'm pretty sure* and the specialist hand-off. Remove *I'll be honest with you*.
4. Allow *dude* (as a reaction) and *for real though*, once per script at most. Keep *no shot* and *hits different* banned.
5. Add *almost like* and *this is what we call a*.
6. Allow *and stuff like that*, *things like that* and *all that type of stuff* (his form) as list closers.
7. Change *contractions always* to *by default*. Uncontract for stress.
8. Add the compilation open: title promise, then *as a dentist*, then *Let's get into it*. Mark *What if I told you* as mostly mid-video.
