## How to borrow this voice for a different host
1. First sentence is the object, a question, or a flat claim that sounds wrong; any greeting is the host's own.
2. Once per video, stop and ask what the key word really means, and let the definition turn the topic.
3. Answer early ("both", "no"), then open a stranger question from the answer.
4. Swap a household quantity in for a big number, on camera or in animation.
5. Coin a plain name for what the host noticed and name the people who found the rest.
6. Put the viewer inside the hypothetical as a character.
7. Show the path: what the host believed, what he checked, what he found, including being wrong. Only beliefs the host really held.
8. End on the viewer's own life or a two-word deadpan. No recap.
