# Voice guide: Cleo Abram (Huge If True)

Reference voice for `/write-script voice:cleoabram`. Structure lives in `docs/huge-if-true-writing.md`; this page is how her sentences sound.

**Evidence.** `profile.md`, `fingerprint.json` (25 long-form, 72,649 words), `exemplars.md`, and seven transcripts read in full: `N0-ZZ9pLyWw` (digging), `fundOLJehWA` (dog), `USmu0scNcSs` (cancer), `pfIWxFIVP_Y` (shoe), `iX6w1P60m8M` (de-extinction), `e3fz3dqhN44` (road trip with a co-host), `LOn-mmezykQ` (fiction plus field trip); openings and endings of all 25. No Shorts in the corpus. Captions include experts and clips; every quote here is her own line. "grep" counts are videos out of 25.

## How she explains a mechanism
- **X is a household Y.** "An x-ray is a shadow picture." `USmu0scNcSs`; "It's like a robot vacuum for ocean cleanup." `iX6w1P60m8M`
- **Scale by something the viewer has done.** "less than 40 km deep which is less than the length of a marathon" `N0-ZZ9pLyWw`
- **Imagine, then a vehicle or recipe** ("imagine" in 20 of 25, grep). "Imagine we go outside and we just start digging." `N0-ZZ9pLyWw`; "Here's the recipe." `iX6w1P60m8M`
- **Plain-word taxonomy.** "four big ways we fight cancer today: cut, poison, burn, melt" `USmu0scNcSs`
- **Expert line, then her translation.** "Basically, that many of the diseases we die from spring in some way from underlying damage" `fundOLJehWA`; "In other words, that dogs as a species age faster than we do when they're young" `fundOLJehWA`. A borrowed analogy gets credit: "quantum computers are not faster cars. They're BOATS." `e3fz3dqhN44`
- **"Which means" as the causal hinge** (13 of 25): "Which means he is roughly halfway through his average lifespan." `fundOLJehWA`

## How she hedges
- Answer, then shrink it: "Turns out the answer is… YES. Or at least, we can try." `fundOLJehWA`
- Flags simplification: "These are general numbers." `USmu0scNcSs`; "I'm paraphrasing here a lot." `yWgHx0HE8m8`
- Declares controversy first: "By the way what I'm doing is controversial." `USmu0scNcSs`
- Plain unknowns: "When will it flip next? We're not sure!" `N0-ZZ9pLyWw`
- Kills the popular version: "This moment is sometimes cited as "leading" to chemo. It didn't." `USmu0scNcSs`
- Says what doesn't exist yet: "There isn't a pill you can buy today that will help your dog live longer. Sorry." `fundOLJehWA`

## How to borrow this voice for a different host
1. Start mid-moment in present tense with the thing in frame and one household-scale comparison in the same breath.
2. Within 30 seconds ask three questions that climb from naive to frontier.
3. Say an old belief and correct it on camera, only one the host really held.
4. Follow every expert or source line with one plain sentence: "Basically..." or "In other words...".
5. Make "we" the protagonist: more "we" than "you".
6. Ask one dumb question per chapter on the viewer's behalf ("how do we know that?").
7. Flag the simplification and the controversy out loud, once each, before a critic does.
8. End on species, then self, then one action; write the outro fresh.
