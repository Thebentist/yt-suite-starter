## The promise (one sentence)
In about 11 minutes you'll check whether you actually have bad breath (a 10-second test you do along with the video), ride from the tip of your tongue down to your tonsils to see where it's really coming from and why it smells like eggs (usually the back of your tongue, not your stomach), and leave knowing what actually fixes it for most people in a couple of weeks, and when it isn't your mouth at all.

## What the viewer expects after the click
A dentist telling them what actually fixes bad breath, fast; a way to know if they even have it; no shaming. Reality beats it with: the wrist check done live, the fact that people are right about their own breath less than half the time (and plenty of worriers don't have it), the tongue as the main source, the mechanism (protein, sulfur, rotten-egg gas), and the honest version of the fixes (what the evidence says about scrapers and alcohol mouthwash, and when to stop blaming your mouth).
