## The story in one line (ABT)
You can't smell your own breath AND everybody blames their stomach, BUT the smell is rotten-egg gas made by bacteria in the one spot nobody cleans, the back of the tongue, THEREFORE the fix is cleaning back there every day, and if two weeks changes nothing, it was never your mouth.

One trip carries the middle: the tip of the tongue → the back (the carpet) → between the teeth and the gums → the spit → down to the tonsils → down the throat to the stomach. Two images: the back of the tongue as a shag carpet; the smell as rotten-egg gas. Repeatable sentence (open, tongue beat, ending; never the same words): it's usually the back of your tongue.
