- **Triple hook, aligned**: text on screen (the most important one, above his head, 2 lines, a specific number where there is one), the first spoken line (subject in sentence one, aimed at "you"), and the first picture all say the same thing.
- **Lock-in zone**: the next 2-4 sentences confirm the hook and give the trust line (orthodontist, clinic proof).
- **Story loops**: context, then a reveal that beats the guess; 3-4 per minute; but/so at every seam.
- **Close the loop**: the last line pays the first (Ben: no cliffhangers); where it can, it loops back to the opening for rewatches.
- **One line people repeat** (the share reason) and no ask in the middle.
