# Phase 2: from "good explainer" to showreel (read this before touching anything)

Ben watched v3 and said the edit must not look cheap: it should look like Cleo Abram's *Huge If True*, and it's a showreel of what an incredible editor can do. He does NOT want stock footage: everything is made in JavaScript. Read `docs/motion-style.md` (still valid) and this file.

## What Cleo's video actually does (studied frame by frame from the 1080p file, `styles/story-hit-1/study/*.jpg`)
- **The picture changes every 1-3 seconds.** Even inside one idea there are hard cuts: wide → close → another angle → a data view. A single composition is almost never held longer than ~3-4 s.
- **~84% of the runtime is visuals**, the host ~16-20%. (Our graphics now cover ~79% of the cut; coverage is fine, *rhythm and look* are not.)
- **Premium, lit, 3D-feeling imagery**: a photoreal globe with an atmosphere glow and a heat map, glowing lines on dark grids, maps in teal/black with clean small-caps labels, archival film with a camera-viewfinder frame. Soft depth of field, bloom on bright things, subtle grain. Nothing looks like flat clip-art.
- **Small print that signals rigour**: "* ARTISTIC RENDERING" top-right on renders, "SOURCE: …" bottom-left on data.

## New tools (already in the runtime)
- **Automatic timing** (use it for every scene): in `defineScene({ anchor: { word: 940, offset: -0.15 }, anchorEnd: { word: 990, edge: 'end', offset: 0.25 }, tail: 0.5, ... })`, then `api.at(i)` = word i's start in scene seconds, `api.atEnd(i)` its end, `api.wordsIn(a, b)`; the duration follows the cut. Special anchors: `anchor: { special: 'wrist-wait', offset: 1.2 }` (+ fixed `duration`). With this, a re-cut only needs a re-render. Drop any hand-loaded cut-words code and hard-coded times. Your plan.json `start`/`end` must equal the scene's `anchor`/`anchorEnd`.
- **Cinematic finish**: call `api.finish(ctx, t, { bloom: 0.3, grain: 0.05, vignette: 0.45 })` as the LAST line of every opaque scene's draw (remove your own grain/vignette so it isn't doubled). Not on transparent overlays.
- **Real 3D (WebGL2, ray-marched SDFs on the GPU)**: `const r = api.gl.create(api, FRAG, { res: 0.5, aa: 1, steps: 110 })` in setup, `ctx.drawImage(r.draw({ uTime: t, ... }), 0, 0, api.W, api.H)` in draw. FRAG defines `float map(vec3 p)`, then `#include <raymarch>`, then `vec3 render(vec2 fc)` (linear colour; tone-mapping and gamma are done for you). Library: noise/fbm, SDF shapes (sphere, ellipsoid, box, capsule, round cone, torus, cylinder), smin/smax, camMat, fresnel, calcNormal, calcAO, softShadow. See `pipeline/motion/gl.js`. `res: 0.5` renders at 1920x1080 and is plenty once bloom/grain are on; frames take ~0.1-0.4 s.
- **The quality bar**: `videos/bad-breath-for-good/scenes/hero/papillae3d.js` and its stills `scenes/out/_look/papillae3d-*.png` (lit 3D papillae: warm key, cool rim, wet specular, subsurface glow, depth fog, bloom). Everything should feel at least that finished.
- **Render queue**: final renders wait for a GPU slot automatically (2 at a time). Iterate with `--stills ... --scale 1` (no queue), render the final once.

## The upgrade checklist (every scene you own)
1. Timing via `anchor`/`anchorEnd`/`api.at` (no hard-coded times).
2. **Rhythm**: any scene longer than ~4 s gets a hard cut to a new framing or composition every 2-4 s (camera jumps to a new angle/zoom in the same world, a switch to a close-up of the detail being named, a data view, then back). Cuts land on words. A viewer should feel it is *edited*, not a slide that animates.
3. **Depth and light**: layer foreground/midground/background, blur the far or near layer (`ctx.filter = 'blur(6px)'` on that layer), light things (gradient + rim + glow), give surfaces texture. No flat clip-art fills.
4. `api.finish(...)` last on opaque scenes.
5. Keep facts exactly as `research-notes.md` says; small SOURCE line on data, "* ARTISTIC RENDERING" (tiny, top-right, `P.faint`) on anatomy/micro renders.
6. Look at stills at every cut point before the final render; fix, look again.
7. Update `plan.json` (anchors = scene anchors, `sfx` cues in scene seconds, `replaces: [...]` if your scene replaces another group's), final 4K render, report.

## Practicalities (same rules as edit/agent-brief.md, which new agents should also read)
- Folders: new groups write only in `scenes/mouth3d/`, `scenes/micro3d/`, `scenes/cgi/` and their own ids in `scenes/out/` and `scenes/out/_look/`. Existing groups keep to their own folder. Nobody edits `pipeline/`, `docs/`, `edit/`, or another group's files. If the runtime or gl.js lacks something, work around it in your folder (a `_lib.js` via `// @use`) and say so in the report.
- Word lookup: `node pipeline/edit/scene-words.mjs --slug bad-breath-for-good --from <w1> --to <w2>`; anchors are the raw word ids it prints (the `i` field of `edit/cut-words.json`).
- Plan entry for a replacement: `{ "id": "scrape3d", "output": "videos/bad-breath-for-good/scenes/out/scrape3d.mp4", "start": {...}, "end": {...}, "transparent": false, "replaces": ["scrape-how"], "sfx": [...] }`. The assembler drops the replaced ids only once your output file exists, so the old version stays as a fallback until then.
- A NEW scene must not overlap Ben's on-camera personality beats outside its words, and must not overlap another full-screen scene: check `node -e` against the other groups' plan.json anchors before choosing your range.
- Quick look: `node pipeline/motion/render.mjs --scene <file> --out videos/bad-breath-for-good/scenes/out/_look/<id> --stills 0.5s,2s,4s --scale 1` (seconds with `s`, or fractions). Final: `node pipeline/motion/render.mjs --scene <file> --out videos/bad-breath-for-good/scenes/out/<id> --pages 3` (waits for a GPU slot; do not pass --no-queue).
- 3D anti-aliasing: `aa: 1` shows stair-stepping on thin bright edges at res 0.5 (see the papillae stills). For hero shots use `aa: 2` or `res: 0.75` if the frame time allows (keep a frame under ~0.5 s).
- Report at the end: each scene id, duration, what it shows, which ids it replaces, anything unsure.
