### Short 2: Tonsil stones (about 60 s)
[VISUAL: a tonsil with two white spots]
Those little white things in the back of your throat that smell terrible, those are tonsil stones. Your tonsils have these little holes in them, and food and gunk get stuck up in there, and every time you swallow it gets squished down into a little stone. One CT study found them in about 1 in 4 people, and most people have no idea. Now please don't go digging at them with a cotton swab or your finger, your tonsils bleed really easily and can get infected, and Dr. James Burns, he's an ENT at Northwestern, basically says don't scrape at them at home at all. Warm salt water, gargle it. And a water flosser can flush them out, but a regular one is way too strong for your throat, it's made to blast stuff off teeth. So lowest setting, come at it from the side, and if it hurts or bleeds, stop. And if they're big or they keep coming back, that's an ENT, they can take them out, and in bad cases they can laser those little holes closed. So yeah, leave the cotton swab out of it.

### Short 3: Your mints aren't fixing it (about 45 s)
Your mints aren't fixing your breath. A mint covers it up for a few minutes, and then it's right back, because the smell isn't coming from your breath really, it's coming from the back of your tongue. Back there it's almost like a shag carpet, and bacteria get down in between and eat all the gunk and they basically poop out sulfur gas, the same gas that makes rotten eggs smell. So a mint is kind of like spraying air freshener in a room you never clean. And if it's a sugary mint, you're feeding the bacteria that cause cavities while you're at it. So get a tongue scraper, scrape the back of your tongue every day, and if you want something in your mouth after you eat, go sugar free gum, xylitol's a good one, it gets your spit going. So probably drop the mints.
