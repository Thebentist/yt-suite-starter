## Edit parameters derived (style.json `edit`)

- Pauses: between sentences keep 0.15 s (cut anything over 0.27 s); inside a sentence keep 0.25 s (cut over 0.4 s). From the median/p90 pause the editor left in.
- Punch-ins: 1.2x on every other segment, hold one framing at most 4 s (p75 shot), no piece shorter than 1.2 s (set after looking; the p10 shot of 0.73 s came from clip swaps and colour bars, not host cuts). Scale set after looking at the sheets (see Visual notes).
- Cut rate target: 6 framing changes per minute, first 60 s also 6 (set after looking; all detected cuts give 21.37 overall and 15 in the first 60 s, but only 6 of those 15 are host jump cuts; see the cut-detector check below).
- Music bed: -14.06 dB clip gain. Deliver at -14.8 LUFS integrated, true peak 0.5 dBTP.
